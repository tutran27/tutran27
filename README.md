### About

I'm an **AI/ML student at Da Nang University of Science and Technology**, based in Da Nang, Viet Nam. My focus is applied AI engineering: translating models and research ideas into usable systems with well-structured pipelines, practical evaluation, and deployment in mind.

I work across **LLM applications, retrieval systems, AI agents, model efficiency, computer vision, and AI-backed services**.

### What I Work On

- **LLM & Agentic Systems** — Tool calling, multi-agent workflows, human-in-the-loop controls, and structured outputs.
- **Retrieval & RAG** — Dense/sparse hybrid search, reranking, evidence grounding, and retrieval evaluation.
- **Model Adaptation & Efficient Inference** — Knowledge distillation, LoRA, quantization, and model serving.
- **Applied Computer Vision** — Object detection and tracking, edge inference, and intelligent monitoring.
